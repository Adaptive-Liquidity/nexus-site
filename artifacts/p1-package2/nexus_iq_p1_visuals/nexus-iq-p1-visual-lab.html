The visual foundation is strong. The information architecture now needs relational depth.

The current routes preserve honesty and clarity, but P1 should expose why a claim is supported, blocked, bounded, or not yet citable—not merely list its status.

Developer / benchmark gap

Link-out surfaces. Methodology and integration choices are stated; neither can yet be explored or rehearsed in the website.

P1.2 · Maturity topology

Show critical paths and bottlenecks—not fake completion percentages.

Topology treats maturity as dependency structure. Foundations can be complete while the composed guarantee remains blocked; this is exactly the distinction the current table cannot express.

Implementation principle

One canonical registry. Four derived instruments.

Claim nodes, maturity status, benchmark provenance, simulator maturity, and limitations should all resolve from structured source data. The visuals must never become a second, manually maintained truth system.

Before publication, add typed claim relationships and benchmark artifact schemas to the canonical content model. Until then, relationship edges and benchmark values remain clearly labeled design fixtures.
